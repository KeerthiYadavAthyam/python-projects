# customer-order-analysis-python
The Customer Order Analysis application helps analyse customer purchasing behavior, their category of interest, and the volume at which they order products. Through this application, the business can be improved by identifying popular products, high-value customers by summing up all the order values, most profitable categories, and by identifying the number of customers who use the application. The key insights are:

1. CUSTOMER BEHAVIOUR: Analyses the pattern of customer orders, their frequent purchase categories and how frequently they use the application to order gives a better idea to understand the customer.

2. ORDER VOLUME: Orders can be analysed if the sales are high or low

3. DIVISION: By analysing the pattern of individual order, we can design an application based on their preferences for each individual
